The Artist

Rooted in the Rift Valley.
Built for the stage.

Patoto Pa Sweetstar is a Kenyan recording and performing artist originating from the Rift Valley region. Born on June 9, 1992, he has established a reputable presence in the music industry through his refined sound, artistic versatility and dedication to high quality musical production.

His work is distinguished by authentic storytelling, cultural depth and a commitment to delivering meaningful and memorable music experiences. From packed lounges to studio sessions, every release and every performance carries that same signature.
